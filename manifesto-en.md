# Manifesto

Hermen@uta begins with a simple unease:

what is happening to language — and, therefore, to us — in the age of artificial intelligence?

We live in a moment when machines produce texts with fluency, coherence, and, at times, beauty.

They do not think.  
But they write.

And that changes everything.

Because if language can function without thought, then perhaps we need to reconsider what we call thinking.

Hermen@uta is an attempt to navigate this territory.

Not as a technical specialist, but as a reader — someone interested in meaning, form, and interpretation.

Here, artificial intelligence is treated neither as a tool nor as a threat.

It is treated as a phenomenon.

A phenomenon that forces us to ask:

how much of what we think is really ours —  
and how much was already inscribed in the language we use?

This is a space to explore that question.

No noise.  
No rush.  
With attention.
